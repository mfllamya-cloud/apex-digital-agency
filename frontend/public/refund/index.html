Refund Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

You can request a refund within 14 days of your purchase. Refunds are processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record, to your original payment method.

1. What you buy

Apex Studio Pro delivers digital goods produced by an automated pipeline: video ads, ad designs, ad copy and hosted landing pages, depending on your package. Deliverables are sent electronically, by download link or hosted URL. Nothing is shipped physically, so there is nothing to return.

2. Refund window

If you are not satisfied with your order, you can request a refund within 14 days of the date of purchase. This applies to every package shown on our pricing page.

3. How to request a refund

  1. Go to paddle.net, find your purchase with the email address used at checkout, and submit a refund request. You can also use the link in your Paddle receipt email.
  2. Or write to us at contact@apexstudiopro.com with your order email and Paddle order number, and we will pass your request to Paddle.

Approved refunds are issued by Paddle to the original payment method. The time for the money to appear depends on your bank or card issuer and is usually 5 to 10 business days.

4. Before you ask for a refund

If a deliverable has a technical fault, for example a file that will not open or a landing page that does not load, tell us first. In most cases we can regenerate or fix it within one business day at no cost. Asking for a fix does not reduce your 14-day refund window.

5. After a refund

When an order is refunded, your licence to use its deliverables ends. You must stop using the refunded assets in your advertising, and any hosted landing page for that order is taken offline.

6. Chargebacks and abuse

Please contact us or Paddle before opening a dispute with your bank; a refund request is faster for everyone. Paddle may decline refund requests where it finds evidence of fraud or abuse of this policy.

7. Your statutory rights

This policy is in addition to, and does not limit, any rights you have under the mandatory consumer law of the country where you live.

8. Contact

Refund and payment questions: paddle.net. Anything else: contact@apexstudiopro.com.
