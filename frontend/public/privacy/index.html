3. Who receives your data

We share personal data only with the parties needed to run the Service:

  • Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record, which processes payments, invoices, taxes and refunds as an independent controller under its own privacy policy.
  • Technical service providers acting on our instructions: Tally (order form), Make (workflow automation), Anthropic, fal.ai and ElevenLabs (AI generation of scripts, images, video and voice), Google (file storage, sign-in and delivery), and Vercel (website hosting).
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your uploads for advertising our own services without your permission.

4. International transfers

Some of these providers are located outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Where personal data is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, the transfer is protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the provider's certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. You can ask us for details.

5. How long we keep data

  • Order details, uploaded files and delivered assets: up to 12 months after delivery, so that we can redeliver or support your order, then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Messages: up to 24 months after the last exchange.
  • Invoicing and tax records: for the period required by law. These are held by Paddle.
  • Technical logs: up to 90 days.

6. Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to our processing, in particular where it is based on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country.

To use these rights, write to contact@apexstudiopro.com. We answer within one month. People in other countries may have similar rights under their local law, and we handle those requests in the same way.

7. Security

We protect personal data with appropriate technical and organisational measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS), access restricted to those who need it, and providers that apply recognised security standards. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you and the competent authority of a data breach where the law requires it.

8. Cookies

This website uses only the cookies and local storage needed for it to work, such as keeping you signed in and remembering your language. The Paddle checkout may set its own cookies needed for payment and fraud prevention. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics or marketing cookies in the future, we will ask for your consent first.

9. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
