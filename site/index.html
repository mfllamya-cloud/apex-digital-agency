FAQ

Questions before you order

Do I really pay no monthly fee?

Yes. You pay once. There is no platform fee and no app stack to rent. Hosting is fast and free for life. With the Flagship store, we connect your own custom domain for free: you add one CNAME record and we do the rest. If you buy a new domain, you pay the registrar directly.

How do I manage my products?

Everything lives in one Google Sheet. Each row is a product: name, price, photo, stock. Change a cell, save, and your live store follows automatically within a couple of minutes. Set Visible to FALSE to hide a product.

Which plan should I choose?

Choose the Cinematic Pack if you already have a store and only need premium content. Choose the Micro-Store if you sell up to 10 products, as a drop-shipper for example. Choose the Flagship Store if you want unlimited products, smart category filters, a slide-out cart, the dual cinematic film system and free custom domain connection.

What do I need to send after I order?

Your brand name and your products with prices and clear photos. For the Cinematic Pack, send your product photos and a short brief about your offer. We take it from there.

Do I own my store?

Yes, outright. Your store, your Google Sheet and your content belong to you. There is no lock-in and nothing to rent from us.

Can I get a refund?

No. Every order is custom work that starts the moment your payment is confirmed, so all sales are final. That is why we invite you to ask us any question before you order, and we will gladly answer it.
