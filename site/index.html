How it works · The magic

Your whole store lives in one Google Sheet.

No complex dashboard. No admin panel to learn. If you can edit a spreadsheet, you can run your store.

  1. I

    We build it and connect your sheet

    Share your brand and products. We design the storefront, link it to your private Google Sheet, and hand you both, ready to sell.

  2. II

    Edit inventory and prices in a row

    Each row is a product. Each column is a detail: name, price, photo, stock. Add an item, change a price or mark something sold out, right in the sheet.

  3. III

    Your live store updates by itself

    Save the sheet and the storefront follows automatically. You keep selling while the technology stays out of your way.
