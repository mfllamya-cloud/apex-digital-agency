FAQ

Questions before you order

What do I need to send?

One to three sharp, well-lit photos of your product or place, your brand name, your offer or key message and the voiceover language. For the Multi-Product Scaler, write one product name per line and add photos for each product. The better the photos, the better the ad.

Do I need to write a script or describe the video?

No. We develop the concept, the shots, the voiceover and the titles from your photos. You only give the business information.

How are the videos made?

The visuals are generated with AI from your real photos, then edited with a voiceover, music and titles. Some platforms ask advertisers to label AI-generated content; we recommend turning that label on when you post.

Will my product look like the real one?

The main shots are built from your photos to keep the shape, colours and label of your product. Small details can vary from shot to shot, which is why clear photos matter.

What is a creative angle?

Two ads for the same product that sell it differently. The first sells the desire and the experience, the second sells the occasion, the benefit or your offer. Testing both shows you which one your audience prefers.

Which formats do I receive?

MP4 files in vertical 9:16 at 1080 × 1920, the format used by TikTok, Instagram Reels, Stories and YouTube Shorts. Copy packs are delivered as text files.
